About

Designed for meaning, not just aesthetics

I'm Mohammad Rishaad, a graphic designer and Computer Science student based in Mangalore, India, working remotely with clients worldwide. I create visual content for Islamic organizations: events, seminars, countdowns, and educational media, where the design has to carry weight before a word is read.

My work spans multiple organizations including Radiant Wing of Huda, Muslim Growth Movement, At-Tayyib Institute, and the Islamic Education Centre, alongside small business work. Each has its own identity, audience, and tone. I design to match each one, not to a template.

Outside of design, I'm studying CS & Business Systems at St. Joseph Engineering College, organizing community programs, and exploring Islamic history and classical scholarship.

How it works

Simple, three steps

01

Brief

You send the details: event, date, speaker, venue, and any text that has to appear. I confirm everything before touching the design.

02

Draft

You get a first concept within 48 hours, then two rounds of revisions to get the layout, type, and tone exactly right.

03

Final files

Print-ready PDF plus sized exports for Instagram, WhatsApp status, and stories. Source file on request.

From $20 USD per poster, or the local equivalent. Course series, campaigns, and bulk sets quoted on request.
