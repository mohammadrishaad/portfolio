About

Designed for meaning, not just aesthetics

I'm Mohammad Rishaad, a graphic designer and Computer Science student based in Mangalore, India, working remotely with clients worldwide. I create visual content for organizations and small businesses: events, seminars, countdowns, menus, and educational media, where the design has to carry weight before a word is read.

My work spans multiple organizations including Radiant Wing of Huda, Tarbiyah Arabic Institute, At-Tayyib Institute, and the Islamic Education Centre, alongside small business work. Each has its own identity, audience, and tone. I design to match each one, not to a template.

Outside of design, I'm studying CS & Business Systems at St. Joseph Engineering College.

What I need from you

Event name, date, time, venue, speaker name and designation, plus any text that has to appear word for word. Logos and photos if you have them. I confirm all of it before starting.

How delivery works

First concept lands within the stated window. Revisions come back within 24 hours. Final files arrive as PNG and JPG, with print-ready PDF and social sizes on Standard and above.

Bigger projects

Course series, Ramadan campaigns, countdown sets, and full brand kits are quoted per project rather than per poster. Message me with the scope and I will send a figure.
