What I need from you

Event name, date, time, venue, speaker name and designation, plus any text that has to appear word for word. Logos and photos if you have them. I confirm all of it before starting.

How delivery works

First concept lands within the stated window. Revisions come back within 24 hours. Final files arrive as PNG and JPG, with print-ready PDF and social sizes on Standard and above.

Bigger projects

Course series, Ramadan campaigns, countdown sets, and full brand kits are quoted per project rather than per poster. Message me with the scope and I will send a figure.

Not sure which one fits?

Describe what you need and I will tell you honestly which package covers it. If none of them do, I will quote the actual job instead of pushing you into a tier.
